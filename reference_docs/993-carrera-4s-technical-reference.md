# Porsche 911 (993) Carrera 4S — Technical Reference Dossier

## Vehicle: 1996 Porsche 911 Carrera 4S
**VIN:** WP0AA2999TS320294  
**Engine:** M64/21 Varioram (3.6L Flat-6, air-cooled)  
**Transmission:** G64/20 6-speed manual with viscous all-wheel drive  
**Color:** Arctic Silver Metallic (570)  
**Production:** 1996 (model year 1995-1998 for 4S)

## Core Specifications

### Engine
- **Type:** 3.6 L air-cooled flat-6 (M64/21 Varioram)
- **Power:** 282 bhp @ 6,100 rpm
- **Torque:** 251 ft lbs @ 5,250 rpm
- **Compression Ratio:** 11.3:1
- **Cooling:** Air-cooled with oil cooler
- **Induction:** Normally-aspirated with Varioram variable-length intake manifold

### Transmission & Drivetrain
- **Transmission:** 6-speed manual (G64/20)
- **Drivetrain:** Permanent all-wheel drive with viscous coupling
- **Final Drive:** 3.25 (variable depending on version)

### Performance
- **0-60 mph:** 5.2 seconds
- **Top Speed:** 168 mph (270 km/h)
- **Weight:** 3,197 lbs (1,450 kg)
- **Height:** 1,315 mm
- **Length:** 4,260 mm
- **Width:** 1,795 mm (wide-body Turbo-look)
- **Wheelbase:** 2,272 mm

### Chassis & Body
- **Body Style:** 2-door coupé
- **Body Kit:** Turbo-look wide-body (M491 option)
- **Suspension:** M030 sport suspension (front: MacPherson struts, rear: multi-link)
- **Brakes:** Brembo 4-piston calipers (inherited from 993 Turbo)
- **Wheels:** 17" or 18" (factory specification)

## 993 Generation Timeline (1994-1998)

### 1994 (MY 1995)
- Launch model with M64/05 naturally-aspirated engine (272 bhp)
- 6-speed manual transmission
- Rear-wheel drive only

### 1995 (MY 1996)
- Introduction of Varioram (M64/21) engine
- Power increased from 272 to 282 bhp
- Torque increased to 251 ft lbs
- Targa variant introduced
- 911 Turbo launched with twin-turbo 3.6L (408 bhp)

### 1996 (MY 1997)
- Carrera 4S launched (your car)
- Features Turbo-look wide-body, Turbo suspension, and brakes
- All-wheel drive standard with viscous coupling
- Engine: M64/21 Varioram (282 bhp, 251 ft lbs)

### 1997 (MY 1998)
- Carrera S introduced (wide-body, RWD only)
- Turbo S launched (424 bhp)
- Carrera 4 production discontinued

### 1998 (MY 1998)
- End of production — final air-cooled 911 generation
- Total production: ~70,000 units across all variants

## Known Issues & Maintenance Notes

### Common Problems
1. **Distributor drive gears** — wear pattern on intermediate shaft
2. **Oil cooler return line seals** — prone to leaking
3. **Hydraulic lifters** — can develop noise over time
4. **Rear main seal (RMS)** — can leak as engine ages
5. **Window regulator failures** — passenger side commonly fails
6. **Climate control** — early units had issues

### Maintenance Intervals
- **Oil change:** Every 10,000 miles or 12 months (whichever comes first)
- **Major service:** Every 30,000 miles (includes V-belt, tensioners)
- **Valve clearance:** Hydraulic tappets (self-adjusting, no manual check needed with Varioram)
- **Transmission fluid:** Every 30,000 miles
- **Cooling system:** Regular oil cooler inspection

### Important Options Codes for 993
- **M491:** Turbo-look wide-body body kit
- **M030:** Sport chassis with stiffer springs and dampers
- **M64/21:** Engine with Varioram (post-1996)
- **G64/20:** 6-speed manual all-wheel drive transmission

## 993 vs. 964 Key Improvements
- **Multi-link rear suspension** (vs. semi-trailing arm)
- **Six-speed transmission** (vs. 5-speed)
- **Improved aerodynamics** (Cd reduced from 0.35 to 0.33)
- **Hydraulic valve lifters** (vs. bucket style requiring adjustment)
- **Dual catalytic converters** (vs. single)
- **More reliable ignition system**
